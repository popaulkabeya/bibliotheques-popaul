# Six Evenings of Family Prayer

*Monday to Saturday, at 9:30 p.m., for 15 minutes. A short prayer, led each evening by Ryan and said together by the family, each from wherever they are. One theme per evening: deliverance, prosperity, success in studies, business and every kind of enterprise, spiritual warfare, protection and healing, and finally thanksgiving.*

### The flow of each evening — 15 minutes

> **1. Opening and agreement — 2 min.** Ryan reads the invocation; each person says their first name and "I agree."
>
> **2. Thanksgiving — 2 min.** Each person gives thanks, in one sentence, for something received during the day.
>
> **3. Reading — 2 min.** Ryan reads the evening's passages slowly.
>
> **4. Prayer and intercession — 5 min.** Ryan reads the evening's prayer, then each person names aloud one person or one situation, in one sentence.
>
> **5. Declaration — 2 min.** Ryan says a line, everyone repeats it.
>
> **6. Closing — 2 min.** The Lord's Prayer together, then "In the name of Jesus Christ. Amen."

### Advice for Ryan

> **Keep to the time.** Set a 15-minute timer. If the intercession runs long, shorten the thanksgiving, not the intercession.
>
> **One sentence per person.** Each person gives one name and one specific situation, without details. A specific prayer receives a specific answer.
>
> **The notebook.** Write down the names mentioned each evening. On Saturday, read the list back before giving thanks. The notebook stays within the family.
>
> **From a distance.** Each person prays from where they are, at the same moment, by phone or video call. What matters is the agreement, not being in the same room.
>
> **The Lord's Prayer.** Said in the version the family knows by heart.

### Sources

> Bible passages: translated from the Louis Segond 1910 French version (public domain), each reference verified before composition. Prayers and declarations: composed for this booklet, based on the passages cited. A booklet of private devotion, composed for one particular family, without ecclesial authority.

# Monday — Deliverance

*9:30 p.m. · 15 minutes*

### 1. Opening and agreement *(2 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, here we are, gathered in your name. You promised: "If two of you agree on earth about anything they ask, it will be granted them by my Father who is in heaven."
>
> **Everyone:** *(their first name)* — I agree.

### 2. Thanksgiving *(2 min)*
*Each person gives thanks for one thing from the day.*

### 3. Reading *(2 min)*

> "When the righteous cry out, the LORD hears, and he delivers them from all their distress." *(Psalm 34:17)*
>
> "If therefore the Son sets you free, you will be truly free." *(John 8:36)*
>
> "… who has delivered us from the power of darkness and transferred us into the kingdom of the Son of his love." *(Colossians 1:13)*
>
> "… he who is in you is greater than he who is in the world." *(1 John 4:4, end of the verse)*

### 4. Prayer and intercession *(5 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, you hear when we cry out, and you deliver us from all our distress. Tonight we bring you what holds us back: fears, bonds, habits, situations that keep returning despite our efforts. We do not ask to endure them, but to be set free from them. He who is in us is greater than what holds us. Bring each person in this family out of the power of darkness.
>
> **Each person, in turn:** names one thing they ask to be delivered from, for themselves or for a loved one.

### 5. Declaration *(2 min)*

> **Ryan, then everyone:** The Son has set us free, we are truly free. What held us back has no more right over us, in the name of Jesus Christ.

### 6. Closing *(2 min)*

> The Lord's Prayer, then: "In the name of Jesus Christ. Amen."

# Tuesday — Prosperity

*9:30 p.m. · 15 minutes*

### 1. Opening and agreement *(2 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, here we are, gathered in your name. You promised: "If two of you agree on earth about anything they ask, it will be granted them by my Father who is in heaven."
>
> **Everyone:** *(their first name)* — I agree.

### 2. Thanksgiving *(2 min)*
*Each person gives thanks for one thing from the day.*

### 3. Reading *(2 min)*

> "The LORD is my shepherd: I shall lack nothing.  
> He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside peaceful waters.  
> He restores my soul, he leads me in the paths of righteousness, for his name's sake.  
> Even when I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I fear no evil, for you are with me: your rod and your staff comfort me.  
> You prepare a table before me, in the presence of my adversaries; you anoint my head with oil, and my cup overflows.  
> Surely goodness and grace will follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the LORD to the end of my days." *(Psalm 23:1-6)*
>
> "Beloved, I wish that you may prosper in every way and be in good health, even as your soul prospers." *(3 John 1:2)*
>
> "It is the blessing of the LORD that makes rich, and he adds no sorrow to it." *(Proverbs 10:22)*
>
> "… I came that the sheep may have life, and have it in abundance." *(John 10:10, end of the verse)*

### 4. Prayer and intercession *(5 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, you want us to prosper in every way, as our soul prospers. We refuse to see lack as a destiny. Bless what is in our hands. May each person have enough to live on, enough to give and enough to set aside. May this family stop lacking and learn to give.
>
> **Each person, in turn:** names one specific need: a job, an awaited payment, an expense to cover, a financial door that is closed.

### 5. Declaration *(2 min)*

> **Ryan, then everyone:** The blessing of the LORD makes rich, and adds no sorrow to it. We were not made to lack.

### 6. Closing *(2 min)*

> The Lord's Prayer, then: "In the name of Jesus Christ. Amen."

# Wednesday — Success

*Studies, business and enterprises · 9:30 p.m. · 15 minutes*

### 1. Opening and agreement *(2 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, here we are, gathered in your name. You promised: "If two of you agree on earth about anything they ask, it will be granted them by my Father who is in heaven."
>
> **Everyone:** *(their first name)* — I agree.

### 2. Thanksgiving *(2 min)*
*Each person gives thanks for one thing from the day.*

### 3. Reading *(2 min)*

> "If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask of God, who gives to all simply and without reproach, and it will be given him." *(James 1:5)*  
> "He who works with a slack hand becomes poor, but the hand of the diligent makes rich." *(Proverbs 10:4)*  
> "May the grace of the LORD our God be upon us! Establish the work of our hands, yes, establish the work of our hands!" *(Psalm 90:17)*  
> "Commit your works to the LORD, and your plans will succeed." *(Proverbs 16:3)*  
> "Let this book of the law not depart from your mouth; meditate on it day and night, so that you may act faithfully according to all that is written in it; for then you will have success in your undertakings, then you will succeed." *(Joshua 1:8)*  
> "… I have set before you an open door, which no one can shut." *(Revelation 3:8, end of the verse)*

### 4. Prayer and intercession *(5 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, we bring you three things. **Our studies:** give those who study knowledge, understanding and wisdom, open their memory, keep them from fear and from laziness. **Our businesses:** give us diligence and skill, send the right clients, the right partners, payments on time, and keep us from dishonesty and from haste. **All our enterprises:** we entrust to you our projects and our steps; open the doors that no one can shut, close those that are not for us, and establish the work of our hands so that what we build will last.
>
> **Each person, in turn:** names one specific matter: an exam, a diploma, a contract, a client, a project or a pending request.

### 5. Declaration *(2 min)*

> **Ryan, then everyone:** God gives me knowledge, understanding and wisdom. The hand of the diligent makes rich. What we entrust to God, we will bring to completion. I can do all things through him who strengthens me.

### 6. Closing *(2 min)*

> The Lord's Prayer, then: "In the name of Jesus Christ. Amen."

>

> "He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High rests in the shadow of the Almighty. I say to the LORD: My refuge and my fortress, my God in whom I trust!" *(Psalm 91:1-2)*
>
> "The LORD will keep you from all evil, he will keep your soul; the LORD will keep your going out and your coming in, from this time forth and forevermore." *(Psalm 121:7-8)*

>
> **Each person, in turn:** names a person to be covered tonight, or a sick person and their specific ailment.

# Saturday — Thanksgiving and Sealing

*9:30 p.m. · 15 minutes*

### 1. Opening and agreement *(2 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, here we are, gathered in your name. You promised: "If two of you agree on earth about anything they ask, it will be granted them by my Father who is in heaven."
>
> **Everyone:** *(their first name)* — I agree.

### 2. Thanksgiving *(2 min)*
*Tonight, Ryan first reads back the names written in the notebook this week. Each person gives thanks for what has already moved.*

### 3. Reading *(2 min)*

> "Shout for joy to the LORD, all you inhabitants of the earth! Serve the LORD with gladness, come into his presence with joy!" *(Psalm 100:1-2)*
>
> "Rejoice always. Pray without ceasing. Give thanks in all things, for this is God's will for you in Jesus Christ." *(1 Thessalonians 5:16-18)*
>
> "May the LORD bless you and keep you! May the LORD make his face shine upon you and be gracious to you! May the LORD lift up his face toward you and give you peace!" *(Numbers 6:24-26)*

### 4. Prayer and intercession *(5 min)*

> **Ryan:** Lord, we give you thanks even before we have seen all the answers, because you had already heard us from the first evening. We thank you for deliverance, prosperity, wisdom in our studies, success in our studies, our businesses and all our enterprises, for victory in the battle, protection and healing. We are not closing this week, we are sealing an agreement for what comes next: may this family keep gathering in your name, not only in emergencies.
>
> **Each person, in turn:** says a word of gratitude for the week, and what they are still waiting for.

### 5. Final declaration *(2 min)*

> **Ryan, then everyone:** If two of us agree, it will be granted to us. We agree, tonight and for what comes next. May God fight for us.

### 6. Closing *(2 min)*

> The Lord's Prayer, then: "In the name of Jesus Christ. Amen."
